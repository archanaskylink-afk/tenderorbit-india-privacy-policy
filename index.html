TenderOrbit India · Android application

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to TenderOrbit India (Android package: com.tenderorbit.india), an independent tender-calculation and eProcurement-website directory application published by Archana Skylink. It explains how information is handled by the application and its third-party services.

1. Information stored on your device

Registration and login are not required. Estimated tender costs, quotation amounts, similar-work amounts, rounding preferences, calculation settings, and procurement portal favourites may be stored locally in the Android app's private storage using SharedPreferences. The app uses this information to perform calculations and restore recent selections or favourites. The developer does not receive these entries through a developer-operated server.

2. Advertising and consent

The application integrates Google AdMob banner advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising is available, Google and advertising partners may process identifiers (including an advertising ID where available), IP address, device and network information, approximate location inferred from device or network signals, advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and related technical data. Depending on applicable consent and settings, this information may be collected, used or shared for advertisement delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

Where required, the app checks advertising-consent status through UMP before requesting advertisements and presents relevant consent choices. When required, an advertising privacy-options entry point is available through the application's Developer Contact section. Debug and unsigned release builds use test advertising; the live banner configuration is used only in appropriately configured signed production releases. The app's calculators and directory can be used without interacting with ads.

For the separate Google services' privacy practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob documentation.

3. External websites and optional promotional content

Choosing an eProcurement portal opens the selected third-party website in a separate Android browser. The browser and destination website may collect IP addresses, cookies, account details, documents, payments, or other information you provide under their own privacy notices. TenderOrbit India does not operate these portals or receive the information that you submit to them.

RCM Associate Buyer information and Woowistic YouTube content are optional third-party promotional sections. If you choose to register, purchase, watch or subscribe, you interact with the appropriate third-party app or website outside TenderOrbit India. No promotional registration, purchase or subscription is necessary to use the tender-calculation tools.

4. Retention and deletion

Locally stored inputs, settings, and favourites remain on your device until you clear TenderOrbit India's app storage or uninstall it. Android backup is disabled for this application. Information processed by external sites, your browser, or Google advertising services is retained and handled according to those providers' applicable privacy practices and controls.

5. Support communications

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, the message, your email address and any information you submit are received through email services and may be used to respond to your request. Support requests may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve the inquiry and comply with applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or payment credentials in support requests.

6. Independence and accuracy

TenderOrbit India is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of any government department, procurement authority, or external portal. Tender criteria and calculator outcomes must be verified against the relevant official tender documents. Third-party sites are operated independently.
